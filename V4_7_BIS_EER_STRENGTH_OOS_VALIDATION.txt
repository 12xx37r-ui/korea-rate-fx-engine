KOREA ENGINE V4.7 — BIS EER / INDEPENDENT KRW-STRENGTH OOS VALIDATION

Scope
- KRW NEER/REER moved to official BIS EER SDMX path: Korea broad nominal M.N.B.KR + real M.R.B.KR.
- One combined BIS request when refresh is due; monthly cache freshness skips BIS calls inside 45 days.
- FRED remains 3 bounded batch requests, Yahoo USD/KRW one request.
  Normal run with fresh BIS cache: 4 external market requests maximum.
  BIS refresh run: 5 maximum. No per-series retry fan-out.
- BIS response is incrementally merged with a 24-month overlap; prior history is retained on failure.
- Independent KRW-strength OOS target added:
  -0.50*log(USD/KRW) + 0.25*log(BIS NEER) + 0.25*log(BIS REER), positive = KRW appreciation.
- 3m/6m/12m expanding-origin prequential candidate selection; candidate losses must mature before selection.
  Zero-change target is the benchmark. OOS score and input coverage are reported separately.
